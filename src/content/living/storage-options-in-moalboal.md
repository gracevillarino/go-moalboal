## There's no self-storage industry here

If you're picturing a climate-controlled unit you can access with a keypad any hour of the
day, recalibrate — Moalboal doesn't have that. There's no self-storage chain, no dedicated
storage compound, nothing you can just book online. What exists instead is a patchwork of
informal arrangements: landlords with a spare room, dive shops with a back locker, and
expat networks passing space along when someone leaves town. Anyone planning a storage
need here should expect to ask around and negotiate directly, not shop a price list.

## Alternatives worth exploring

Since there's no formal option to default to, these are the workarounds people actually use:

- **Landlord-arranged storage** — a spare room or ground-floor bodega, usually tied to an
  existing or past rental agreement rather than offered as a standalone service
- **Dive shop or resort lockers** — for gear specifically, and typically only extended to
  active customers or regulars, not the general public
- **Container or warehouse rental near the highway** — aimed at businesses moving stock,
  not individuals with a few boxes, and likely to require a larger minimum commitment
- **Informal expat community swaps** — space offered through Facebook groups or word of
  mouth when someone is traveling or between leases

## Things to check before committing

- Security: locks, CCTV, who else has access
- Weather exposure: humidity, flooding risk, pest control
- Cost basis: per month, per box, or per square meter
- Access hours and whether a deposit or contract is required
