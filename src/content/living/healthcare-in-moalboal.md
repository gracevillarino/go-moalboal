Moalboal has primary healthcare, pharmacies, clinics and a government Rural Health Unit, while more complex diagnostics, specialist treatment and major emergencies may require travel to a larger facility elsewhere in Cebu. The most useful preparation is knowing that boundary before you need it.

## Build a local care map

Locate the Rural Health Unit, a nearby pharmacy, dental care and any clinic relevant to your household. Visit during ordinary hours to confirm services and current contact details. A map listing does not prove that a doctor, medicine, laboratory service or vaccine is available that day.

The municipality has also reported plans for expanded urgent and ambulatory services through a proposed BUCAS facility. Treat planned capacity as planned—not as a service available until an official opening and operating scope are confirmed.

## Know the escalation route

Ask a local clinician which larger hospital is appropriate for trauma, cardiac symptoms, stroke, serious infection, obstetric emergencies or specialist care. Travel time depends on the destination, traffic, weather and available transport. Save more than one transport contact.

For diving-related illness, call emergency services and the dive operator's emergency contact immediately. Do not attempt to solve possible decompression illness through a normal clinic visit or by travelling without professional direction.

## Prepare a household health file

Keep a secure paper and digital record containing:

- full names, dates of birth and emergency contacts;
- allergies, diagnoses and current medicines;
- insurance and identification details;
- preferred hospital and transport contacts;
- vaccination records where relevant;
- the location of passports and funds for urgent payment.

Visitors and foreign residents should understand how their insurer handles pre-authorisation, direct billing, evacuation and reimbursement. Keep enough accessible funds for care or transport that may require payment before an insurance claim.

## Heat, water, animals and the sea

Dehydration, sun exposure, cuts, gastrointestinal illness and scooter injuries are ordinary risks worth planning against. Use safe drinking water, clean wounds promptly and seek medical advice for animal bites or scratches; rabies prevention is time-sensitive and should not be postponed.

Water activities require their own discipline. Use reputable operators, disclose health conditions, follow no-fly guidance after diving and never mix alcohol with an activity that depends on judgement and physical response.

## Verify the emergency list regularly

Phone numbers, clinic hours and service availability change. Keep a printed list at home and review it every few months. Include your barangay, nearby landmarks and a map pin so another person can clearly describe where help is needed.

> The goal is not to turn every illness into a trip to Cebu City. It is to know which problems can safely stay local and which cannot wait.

This guide is general planning information, not medical advice. Contact a qualified health professional for individual decisions and use emergency services for urgent symptoms.
